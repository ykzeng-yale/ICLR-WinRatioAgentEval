\paragraph{Interactive open-weight airline workflows.}
On 49 $\tau^2$-bench tasks with two trials per arm, Qwen2.5-7B and
Qwen3-4B each succeeded on 15/98 units. All 196 planned entries are
retained, including two infrastructure failures. Appendix~\ref{app:open_airline}
reconciles 206 attempts, a serving-policy amendment, descriptive contrasts
and a masked-replay illustration. These batch observations do not establish
equivalence or live stopping gains. The earlier incomplete telecom pilot
is retained in Appendix~\ref{app:pilot}.
