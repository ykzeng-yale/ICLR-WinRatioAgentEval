\paragraph{Open-weight coding workflows.}
On 591 tasks, local Qwen2.5-Coder-7B with self-tests and repairs (B) and
single-shot generation (A) each passed 433 tasks. B used 4.46 times the
mean workflow latency and 6.33 times the total tokens. The 295 randomized
first-pass cross-task pairs gave 69 B wins, 18 ties and 208 losses under
success, latency, then completion tokens. Net benefit was $-0.471$;
the post-hoc normal-mixture band $[-0.654,-0.288]$ targets running
history-conditional means, not a fixed roster, without selection adjustment.
All 1,182 episodes completed; the success guardrail was not certified.
Appendix~\ref{app:open_coding} provides details and descriptive same-task results.
