\paragraph{Open-weight coding workflows.}
On 591 tasks (427 MBPP and 164 HumanEval), a local Qwen2.5-Coder-7B
workflow with self-tests and up to two repairs (B) and single-shot generation
(A) each passed 433 tasks; B used 4.46 times the mean workflow latency and
6.33 times the total tokens. Randomized first-pass exposure yielded 295
cross-task pairs: 69 B wins, 18 ties and 208 losses under success, then
latency, then completion tokens. Net benefit was $-0.471$.
A post-hoc application of the fixed normal-mixture construction gave
$[-0.654,-0.288]$ for the running average of history-conditional pair means;
this is not fixed-roster inference or adjusted for analysis selection.
All 1,182 episodes were completed, and the separately analyzed success
guardrail was not certified. Appendix~\ref{app:open_coding} gives the design,
resource accounting, marginal-band scope and descriptive same-task analysis.
An earlier incomplete telecom feasibility pilot is retained separately in
Appendix~\ref{app:pilot}.
