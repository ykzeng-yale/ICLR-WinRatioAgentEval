## Scientific disposition

**Do not accept the current instrument as certification of continuous active load.** Its arrival-window arithmetic is a useful scripted diagnostic, but the state it observes differs from the state the coverage contract requires. This is an instrumentation/design mismatch, not a counterexample to win-statistics inference and not an unfavorable experiment result.

`lab_load.py:23–53,123–207` interprets client stream-arrival times as evidence of production at those instants and then connects arrivals separated by at most 0.5 seconds. A server may finish producing before buffered events reach the client. Even with no buffering, output at two instants does not establish activity throughout the intervening interval. The splitting rule identifies gaps in **observed arrival traffic**, not gaps in compute activity. Declaring the maximum gap in `lab_prepare.py:419–450` makes the assumption explicit but cannot establish it. An attempt entirely between two arrivals can still receive valid coverage although no activity was observed during the attempt.

`JitterProbe.bound` at `lab_load.py:284–308` measures a different thread's empirical maximum wake-up lateness. Neither 20 samples, the 0.010-second floor, nor preregistration makes it a bound on delivery/observation error for each window endpoint. The code/documents acknowledge missing buffering control; that limitation is substantive. Replacing the empirical maximum with an arbitrary larger constant would not repair the identification problem.

## Concrete source and offline findings

1. **Two-worker load is not enforced by union coverage.** The specification prescribes two concurrent generations, whereas `_coverage_verdict` accepts the union of windows: that verifies at least one window covers a time, not two distinct concurrent loads. The saved single-generation case expressly passes. `StreamingHttpLoad._loop` also names its generations `gen_000000`, etc., with no per-source prefix. Two independently instantiated sources routed into one observer would collide in generation identity unless the composition layer adds a source ID. These are implementation requirements for the proposed two-source regime, not an additional sample-size request.
2. **Unhealthy-source claim is not enforced.** `observe` records `source_healthy` but does not refuse when it is false (`lab_load.py:560–603`). A pure offline witness with one scripted generation, five arrivals from100.0 to100.4, 20 injected jitter samples, `healthy=False`, and attempt[100.12,100.18] yielded `active=True` and `coverage.valid=True`. This contradicts the module's claim that unhealthy sources yield no certifiable windows. In a lifecycle implementation preserve historical evidence, but do not certify an attempted interval crossing a failed or unresolved lifecycle from an optimistic health flag.
3. **Events are not necessarily tokens.** `_one_generation` at `:407–435` accepts every non-DONE `data:` line without parsing its contents. A two-line mock response containing only role metadata and an empty choices/usage event produced two “arrivals.” No HTTP request occurred. Keep stream events as diagnostics, parse content if reporting token arrival counts, and do not state `max_tokens=1024` guarantees exactly1024 emitted tokens or identical work to another transport; it is a cap and requests may terminate earlier.

## Finite repair and design decision

Use a **pre-outcome operational offered-load definition**, not an unachievable assertion of continuously busy hardware: require two distinct, server-acknowledged in-flight decoding lifetimes/slot occupancies throughout each verifier interval. Record their entry/exit events on the same host monotonic clock as verifier start/end, using pinned server instrumentation or an explicit server-side lifecycle acknowledgment. Build intervals from those lifecycle events and require concurrency **at least two**, with distinct source/request identities. Client POST-to-response intervals alone are insufficient because queued work and buffered completion differ from server-side lifetime. Server scheduling pauses inside that defined occupancy are part of the operational regime; do not claim100% GPU utilization or trial-load equivalence from them.

Retain token traffic and timer jitter as diagnostics. No arbitrary gap/endpoint tolerance is needed to turn discrete observations into a theorem. If server lifecycle evidence is unavailable, report that limitation and do not let this arrival instrument satisfy the present continuity contract; root may explicitly amend the intended operational load definition before outcomes. This is one finite instrumentation/specification repair, not a request for additional model grids, repeated full tests, or indefinite calibration.

The proposed finite specification already gives two servers, one start attempt each, zero restarts, smoke/reference/calibration steps, and labels the transport/tolerance choices as pending. Preserve those bounds. Withdraw its categorical claim that one generator necessarily biases exclusions in a known direction: contention can change runtimes, but the sign and magnitude of downstream exclusion differences are not established here. Root should reconcile its operative episode budget with the already authoritative effective plan rather than infer new execution authority from this draft.
