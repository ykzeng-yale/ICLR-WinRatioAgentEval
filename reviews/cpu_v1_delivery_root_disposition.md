### D. Do not turn distinct planning quantities into universal sample-size claims

The [independent power review](power_diagnostic_theory_review.md) finds useful arithmetic but unsupported interpretations. Given the reported KL .001149011, alpha .00625, an admissible iid joint-law model and **80% power target**, binary-KL data processing gives a necessary real-valued horizon 3099.1788, hence at least **3,100 integer pairs**. Power values .2351 and .1485 are conditional upper bounds, not demonstrated attainable maxima. Deliver exact null/alternative laws and information available to the procedure before using the bound: score-only KL need not bound a method observing richer traces, and without-replacement pairing is not automatically a product experiment.

**6,697** exactly reproduces a fixed-mixture crossing evaluated on fractional expected pilot counts under one illustrative law. It is neither demonstrated 80% power nor an expected stopping time. **17,097** is the normal-radius crossing on an exactly zero observed-mean path, not a guarantee over all outcomes or a sufficient powered horizon. The reported **22.6%** log gap is not a proved irreducible cost of monitoring; it compares different quantities and includes no optimality argument. Equal pilot success counts do not prove equal true performance. Historical retrospective RETAIN calculations are not prospective decisions made during collection. None of these new claims enters the paper as established evidence.

Preserve the current predictable-range normal-mixture proof. An empirical-variance substitution requires its own valid argument; this does not invalidate every variance-adaptive method. The reported drift miscoverage, generic hedged-capital diagnostics and numeric success probabilities lack deposited inputs/code/outputs. Supply their exact calculations if the claims are retained; do not launch a new broad grid to rescue them. Previously excluded contributed methods remain excluded.
