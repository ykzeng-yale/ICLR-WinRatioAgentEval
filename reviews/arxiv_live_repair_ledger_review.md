The ordinary trial reader raises `ChainError: segment numbering has gaps: missing [0]`. Exact output: `work/live_repair_ledger/coverage_witness.json` and `independent_checks.log`.

**Required follow-up:** reconcile seed-check coverage with trials actually opened in the program chain. For an opened trial whose chain is missing, empty, or unreadable, emit a visible coverage/integrity finding and avoid representing cross-trial uniqueness as fully established. Keep legitimate seed collisions at the existing DEFECT severity; missing evidence is a separate issue. Add negative fixtures for the unreadable-chain witness and missing opened-trial path. Future trials not yet opened should remain distinguishable from missing evidence. A separate per-trial audit could detect this damage, but `verify_program` currently does not convey that its own seed comparison omitted the trial.
