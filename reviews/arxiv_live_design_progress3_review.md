Clarify whether this cap applies per trial and how tasks are reused or partitioned across the four trials. “Not observed in this study's pilot” is the supportable provenance claim to check; it does not establish that a public task was absent from model training or other external use.

2. **Stratum reporting and target:** freeze the stratum enrollment order, pooled target/weight convention and roles of separate S1/S2 summaries. Descriptive subgroup summaries fit the existing plan. Additional simultaneous subgroup confidence/decision claims need an explicit error allocation; the current four-trial, two-band-per-trial budget does not automatically cover added subgroup families. This is a clarification of proposed reporting, not a finding that undisclosed subgroup intervals are already wrong or a request for more trials.

3. **The new latency endpoint:** the reported two-tier rule matches the offered comparator form if `cost` is explicitly defined as `latency_s`, success is first, cost is eligible only for joint success, and exact 5% equality is a tie. Freeze the clock's start/end boundaries and use that same latency definition in pilot recomputation, live comparison and partial-enclosure certificates. For a fixed successful A latency `a` and a pending B latency lower bound `b`, `b > a/(1-.05) = a/.95` is a sufficient strict early-A-win condition under monotone latency and the stated eligibility rule; equality alone is not. Verify this constant and boundary fixture after changing the prior 10% rule. The pending B can then either fail (A wins on success) or succeed at a sufficiently larger latency (A wins on the second tier). This does not require a new dataset or simulation panel.
