## Score justification

The mathematics is correct: every main-text theorem and the new Appendix B results re-derive cleanly, and the reported numbers are exact replays of the code. The paper is unusually careful about estimands and about what its guarantees do not cover. But judged by ICLR standards the contribution is a correct assembly of known pieces (GPC kernel + HT pair randomization + Hoeffding normal-mixture CS + fixed-grid betting + IUT + worst-case enclosures), each with a close published precedent that the manuscript does not name, and the two claims that would lift it above that (a usable drift guarantee and a general delay speed-up) are respectively untested and largely determined by a simulator parameter. Weak-reject / marginally below threshold (5): sound, but the novelty positioning and the evidence for the headline claims need the concrete changes above; items 1-3 are fixable before the deadline without new theory, item 2's experiment is a small addition to run_stress_tests.py-style code.
