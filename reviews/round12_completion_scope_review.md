## 2. What the retained paper actually establishes

The main text presents a design-specific protocol built from established win-statistic and sequential-inference machinery, not a new generic confidence-sequence inequality. It distinguishes paired-task and cross-arrival targets, component requirements, stationary and drifting guarantees, enrollment-prefix partial-score bounds, and the richer calendar-time filtration. It explicitly does not establish universal efficiency against all sequential U-statistic methods.

The empirical claims have corresponding evidence:

- **Synthetic operating characteristics:** the stated generators, repetition counts, targets, comparison conventions and uncertainty support the reported rates and sample use. The all-pairs projection-Gaussian comparator is labeled asymptotic; a component-gate rejection is not relabeled as guarded deployment error. The PR7 review does not claim an independent rerun of all 56,000 main/calibration replicates. The PR10 audit did rerun its complete stated panel.
- **Partial-trace feasibility:** the synthetic delay result and historical ordinal certificate audit support their specified schedules. They do not establish measured concurrent production latency savings. The current abstract, methods and limitations preserve this boundary.
- **Coding:** one physically collected randomized first-pass laboratory schedule provides observed workflow scores and resources. The retained normal-mixture analysis is post hoc, targets running history-conditional means, and makes neither a full-roster nor joint 95% claim. Same-task scores are descriptive; the success guardrail remains uncertified. The reported equal overall success counts do not establish equivalence.
- **Airline:** fresh local outcomes were collected all A then all B, including an outcome-informed operational amendment. The retained analysis is descriptive. The optional orientation illustration conditions on the full collected array and fixed matching under a nominal independent-coin model; its target is already known from the array. It is not fresh-run or population inference. All 196 canonical units remain, including two empty failure placeholders, with all 206 attempts separately accounted for.

These are scientifically coherent bounded claims. Weak positive evidence, an abstaining guardrail, incomplete pilot collection, and a negative workflow result do not require a favorable rerun to count as completed studies. The paper cannot infer broader effectiveness from those limitations; it already says so.
