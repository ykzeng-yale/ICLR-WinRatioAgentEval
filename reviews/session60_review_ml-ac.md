## Justification (calibrated to ICLR)

Sound, careful, and honest, but the evidence for the claim in the title is not yet in the paper. The theory is a correct specialization of Howard et al., Waudby-Smith and Ramdas, Berger-style IUT, and Even and Josse, plus one modest but real design-specific addition (the hierarchy-aware prefix envelope). At ICLR the paper will be judged on whether the protocol changes agent-evaluation decisions and whether it is compared against valid alternatives. Today the main text compares a valid method against an invalid one, reports real agent data only descriptively, and leaves on disk the very experiments (replay streams, a valid multinomial competitor, CS widths, a decision-disagreement matrix, HAL latency) that would answer the AC's questions. With those integrated, the related-work gaps closed, and the presentation reworked around one figure and one operational statement per theorem, I would expect 6 from most reviewers and 8 from a sympathetic one.
