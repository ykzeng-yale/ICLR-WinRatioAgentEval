The first 60 points are verified baseline assets, not certification of unexecuted extensions. A substantive defect can reopen an earned milestone. Future progress is awarded in the stated five-point increments, not for an uncommitted draft, running process or uploaded file alone. The pending protocol/adapter review remains a prerequisite for live execution even though the collection points are awarded only after completion. CPU deterministic clearance precedes the trial; the full calibration panel must be accepted before calibration claims enter the paper.

Favorable, significant or deployment-positive results are never required. A completed prespecified trial with validated abstention is valid evidence. An infeasible study must receive an explicit scope decision and be reported transparently; it cannot silently receive completion points or be replaced after observing an unfavorable effect.
