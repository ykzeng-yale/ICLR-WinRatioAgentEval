\section{Exploratory power and certificate diagnostics}
\label{app:power_diagnostics}
The calibration panel in Appendix~\ref{app:t1_validation} does not locate the
transition from abstention to deployment. Two additional synthetic panels vary
the hierarchy mean while holding the success mean at $0.20$, the success
noninferiority margin at $0.03$, and the enrollment horizon at 2,000 pairs.
They use the same tick-batched observation convention and guarded ADAPTER rule.
Non-informative (N) and informative (A) delay cells have separate random streams;
the constructions within a trial share a path. The coarse panel contains
80,000 trial paths (namespace 3); a subsequent 48,000-path panel (namespace 4)
uses rungs chosen after the coarse outcomes were inspected. The latter is an
outcome-informed exploratory follow-up, not an independently prespecified
extension of a single eleven-rung experiment. The T1 null cells have different
outcome laws and success means and are not appended as zero-effect points.

At hierarchy mean $0.10$, the coarse N and A proportions are $52.65\%$ and
$84.525\%$. In the finer panel, A increases from $1,885/4,000=47.125\%$ at
$0.08$ to $2,681/4,000=67.025\%$ at $0.09$; marginal Wilson 95\% Monte Carlo
intervals are $[45.582,48.674]\%$ and $[65.552,68.465]\%$.
N is $1,284/4,000=32.1\%$ at $0.09$, compared with the coarse $52.65\%$
at $0.10$. These observed proportions straddle $50\%$ at adjacent measured
settings; they are not confidence bounds for a unique continuous crossing or a
minimum detectable effect. Figure~\ref{fig:power_diagnostics} reports all measured
rungs while keeping the two panels separate. The intervals are pointwise Monte
Carlo summaries, without simultaneous or post-selection coverage. They do not
inherit anytime validity from the monitoring rule.

\paragraph{Paired certificate ablation.}
A focused post-hoc intervention disables elapsed-cost narrowing in exactly-one-revealed-success states,
while retaining failure/pending/both-final constraints and the success bounds and original namespace-3 draws at hierarchy
means $0.05$ and $0.10$. It reuses 32,000 unique trial coordinates for matched original/disabled
contrasts. Two disabled-arm attempts evaluated those same coordinates twice
(64,000 execution evaluations); the duplicate attempt adds no independent sample. The four selected cells were chosen after inspecting the
coarse panel.

The original-minus-disabled change in the A-minus-N deployment gap is
$+0.125$ percentage points at $0.05$ (approximate pointwise 95\% Monte Carlo
interval $[-0.0298,0.2798]$) and $-13.25$ points at $0.10$
($[-14.0177,-12.4823]$). Thus disabling certificates \emph{widens} the gap at
$0.10$. Certificates contribute deployments within all four recorded cells,
especially N at $0.10$, but the tested intervention does not support the proposed
explanation that they account for the A-over-N advantage. This heterogeneous
component contribution neither refutes the enclosure theory nor establishes a
universal benefit from informative delay. Interval standard errors use paired
trial differences within each cell and independent-cell variance addition for
the gap change, under the specified separate trial-stream model. No program-level
latent variable is shared by its four generated trials.

\paragraph{Provenance and reproduction limits.}
The coarse and fine panels have retrospective driver/law-source binding. A coarse
failed-attempt directory was deleted; its original source state, timestamps,
observation counts and resource usage cannot be recovered. The fine panel retains
its initial failed attempt, but its deposited source/namespace receipt required a
retrospective correction (actual namespace 4 versus a namespace-3 receipt field).
The ablation inherits the coarse provenance limitation; both ablation attempts
are retained, but the first attempt's complete driver blob and output-cap evidence
were not recovered. Later hashes do not prove which bytes ran earlier. These
limitations are not repaired by adding results to this paper.

Numerical checks reconcile the saved coordinates, hashes, deployment counts and
Monte Carlo summaries; they do not regenerate latent paths or independently
re-execute the complete scientific implementation. The release includes unchanged
primary records for the coarse and fine panels and the corrected disabled arm,
with exact repository pins and a standard-library saved-record reproducer.
Surviving failed-attempt records and provenance addenda remain linked at those snapshots.
The duplicate ablation attempt is preserved in the repository and is excluded
from aggregation. These synthetic diagnostics supply no new live-agent outcomes,
measured production savings, prospective confirmation or human signoff.
