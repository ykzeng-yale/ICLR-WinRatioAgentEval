## Scientific conclusion

The route remains promising for an explicit operational question: whether a workflow improves a priority-ordered outcome while meeting separately stated component safeguards, including when some outcomes are pending. The established statistical tools support meaningful guarantees under declared targets and observation assumptions. The new audit does **not** support universal power, a production-efficiency claim, or novelty from win statistics alone. Some observed abstention reflects a deliberately demanding success guard and conservative intervals; some incoming implementation and interpretation defects are real and separable. An experiment should distinguish these explanations, not be redesigned until deployment becomes significant.

Existing simulations, historical replay and open-weight pilots remain separate evidence layers. New v1 CPU output is received and partially reconciled, but is not accepted as validation of the updated live rule.
